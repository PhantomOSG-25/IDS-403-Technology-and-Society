# Provenance and privacy

## Source basis

This portfolio adaptation is based on the verified IDS-403 activity and project documents in the private schoolwork collection. Those materials explore technology through social-science, human-centered, and responsible-technology perspectives, using a tablet-based pest-control field-management scenario.

## Adaptation boundary

The repository presents an original, condensed case-study narrative and analysis framework. It does not claim that the source coursework contained software implementation, production requirements, or empirical field measurements. Recommendations and success measures are portfolio framing added to make the analysis useful to a professional audience.

## Privacy decisions

- Original DOCX submissions are not included.
- Instructor, course-submission, and personal metadata are not included.
- No customer, employer, or operational records are included.
- The scenario is discussed at a systems level without confidential business details.

## Citation practice

The private source documents contain references to NIST, Pew Research Center, USDA, EPA, NetSuite, Davis, and other sources. Any future public expansion should preserve accurate attribution and re-check current URLs before publication.
